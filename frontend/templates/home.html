Support & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating the Container Virtual Labs platform.

Do students need Docker Desktop installed on their local computers?
No! Our Virtual Lab suite includes a dual execution engine. If Docker is running locally on the machine, real container commands execute. If Docker is closed or not installed, our Virtual Sandbox Engine seamlessly simulates all command executions, container outputs, and verifications.
How do students log into the virtual lab portal?
Students can log in using their assigned Student ID (e.g. STU001) or registered email address. Faculty members can manually enroll students or bulk-import student rosters from Excel spreadsheets, which automatically generates default credentials.
How is student lab progress saved?
Progress is tracked step-by-step and automatically stored in the local SQLite relational database (vlab.db). When a student completes tasks or passes verification tests, their progress updates in real-time across both student and faculty dashboards.
Can faculty members upload lecture videos and course materials?
Yes! The Faculty Control Center allows instructors to publish video lecture URLs for each lab module, review support tickets submitted by students, download credential spreadsheets, and view detailed progress analytics.
